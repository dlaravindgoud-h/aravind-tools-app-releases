## 📱 How to Install on Android
1. Click the direct download link on your Android phone to download the `.apk` file:
   **[https://github.com/dlaravindgoud-h/aravind-tools-app-releases/raw/main/2026-Sep-03%2017-24/aravind-tools.apk](https://github.com/dlaravindgoud-h/aravind-tools-app-releases/raw/main/2026-Sep-03%2017-24/aravind-tools.apk)**
2. Open your device's **Downloads** folder and tap **aravind-tools.apk**.
3. If prompted with *"For your security, your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps"*, tap **Settings** and enable **"Allow from this source"**.
4. Tap **Install** and launch the app.
