## 📱 How to Install on Android
1. Click the download link above on your Android phone to download the `.apk` file.
2. Open your device's **Downloads** folder and tap **aravind-tools.apk**.
3. If prompted with *"For your security, your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps"*, tap **Settings** and enable **"Allow from this source"**.
4. Tap **Install** and launch the app.
